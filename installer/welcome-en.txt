Game Optimizer — setup wizard

This app keeps running games smoother: high priority, protected memory,
and optional RAM reclaim from large apps such as Chrome.

Games are never closed. Administrator authorization is required to install.
Files and preferences remain in the current Windows profile
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\GameOptimizer). If credentials for another account are used,
per-user data may belong to that account.

Next you can choose the folder, a desktop shortcut, and whether the app
should start with Windows.
